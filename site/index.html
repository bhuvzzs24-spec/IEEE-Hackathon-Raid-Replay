Raid Replay

230 million people watch Pro Kabaddi, yet no open sports-data standard could describe a single raid. We built the first kabaddi plugin for IPTC Sport Schema, and a converter that goes there and back without losing a single value. Below is the final of our simulated league, as the converted data tells it.

How it works

One raid, three formats, zero loss.

Our simulator writes native kabaddi JSON. The converter (Python + rdflib) maps every field to Sport Schema and back. pySHACL checks the result against IPTC's shapes plus ours.
