How it works

One raid, three formats, zero loss.

Our simulator writes native kabaddi JSON. The converter (Python + rdflib) maps every field to Sport Schema and back. pySHACL checks the result against IPTC's shapes plus ours.

Data handling

Messy in, clean out - and nothing lost on the way.

We inject realistic problems into the simulated matches (seeded): numeric strings, padding, wrong-case codes, nulls, unknown keys, duplicates, swapped events, missing fields, unknown codes, broken rules. The pipeline fixes what it can with a logged reason and quarantines the rest - never silently. Every published match round-trips with 0 differences.
